About Us

Who We Are

YAD MUN (Youth Ambassadors in Diplomacy & Model United Nations LBG) is a youth-focused organization dedicated to leveraging Model United Nations simulations to develop responsible leaders.

Through diplomacy education, Model United Nations, leadership programmes, and international partnerships, we provide young people with opportunities to learn, participate, and lead.

Our work connects the classroom with real-world international affairs.

Our Mission

To educate, connect and empower young people through diplomacy education, Model United Nations, leadership development, civic engagement, policy dialogue and international exposure.

Our Vision

To become a leading platform for developing ethical, informed and globally minded young leaders capable of contributing meaningfully to national, regional and international development.
